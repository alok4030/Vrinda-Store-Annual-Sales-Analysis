# 📊 Vrinda Store Sales Analysis Dashboard

![Dashboard Preview](Vrindass)

## 🚀 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing **30,000+ e-commerce sales transactions** for Vrinda Store and transforming raw business data into an interactive Excel dashboard that delivers actionable business insights.

The dashboard helps stakeholders understand:
- Customer purchasing behavior
- Top-performing sales channels
- Product performance
- Regional sales trends
- Seasonal revenue patterns

The primary goal of this project was to convert messy operational data into meaningful insights that support smarter business decisions.

---

# 🎯 Business Problem

Vrinda Store sells products through multiple online platforms, but decision-makers lacked visibility into critical business areas such as:

- Which channels generate the highest revenue?
- Who are the most valuable customers?
- Which product categories perform best?
- Which states contribute the most sales?
- How do sales trends fluctuate over time?

Without proper analysis, it becomes difficult to optimize:
- Marketing campaigns
- Inventory planning
- Regional targeting
- Customer acquisition strategies

This project addresses those challenges through data-driven analysis and dashboard reporting.

# ⚙️ Project Workflow

## 1️⃣ Understanding the Business Problem
Identified key business questions related to customer behavior, sales performance, and channel profitability.

## 2️⃣ Data Cleaning
Performed preprocessing to improve data quality:

- Standardized data formats

---

## 3️⃣ Data Transformation
Created additional fields to support analysis:

## 4️⃣ Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

The analysis helped uncover customer patterns, high-performing regions, and revenue drivers.

- KPI Metrics
- Slicers
- Dynamic Filtering
- Visual Trend Analysis

The dashboard allows users to explore insights interactively and make faster business decisions.

# 📈 Key Insights

## 👩 Customer Demographics
Women contributed approximately **65% of total sales**, making them the most valuable customer segment.

The **30–49 age group** showed the highest purchasing behavior, indicating a strong target audience for marketing campaigns.

---

## 🛒 Top Performing Sales Channels
Amazon, Myntra, and Flipkart generated the majority of revenue, highlighting the importance of online marketplaces in overall sales performance.

---

## 👗 Best-Selling Product Category
The **Set** category emerged as the highest-selling product type across all categories.

---

## 🌍 Regional Performance
Top-performing states:
- Maharashtra
- Karnataka
- Uttar Pradesh

Together, these regions contributed significantly to overall revenue generation.

---

## 📅 Seasonal Trends
Sales followed noticeable seasonal patterns, creating opportunities for:
- Targeted promotions
- Festive campaigns
- Inventory optimization
